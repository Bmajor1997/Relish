- 6 | oz | raw shrimp, peeled, deveined and roughly chopped
- 1 | tbsp | light soy sauce
- 1 | tsp | toasted sesame oil, plus more to serve
- 1 | | large egg white
- 1 | tsp | grated fresh ginger
- 1/2 | tsp | salt
- 1/2 | tsp | sugar
- 1/4 | tsp | ground white pepper
- 30 | | square egg wonton wrappers
- 6 | cups | low-sodium chicken broth
- 3 | | thin slices of fresh ginger

- 2 | | scallions, thinly sliced
- | | salt, to taste
# Put the pork, shrimp, soy sauce, sesame oil, egg white, grated ginger, salt, sugar and white pepper in a bowl. Stir hard in one direction for 2 minutes, until sticky.
# Place 1 tablespoon of filling in the center of a wrapper. Wet the edges, fold it in half into a triangle and press out the air. Bring the two side corners together, overlapping, and press to seal. Keep the finished wontons under a damp towel.
# Bring the broth and ginger slices to a simmer in a pot and season with salt.
# Bring a separate large pot of water to a boil. Cook the wontons in batches for 3 to 4 minutes, until they float and the filling is cooked through (160°F).
# Add the bok choy to the broth for the last 2 minutes.
# Divide the wontons and bok choy between bowls, ladle the hot broth over and top with scallions and a few drops of sesame oil.

T Stirring the filling in one direction makes it springy and juicy.
S Freeze uncooked wontons on a floured tray, then bag them. Cook from frozen for 5 to 6 minutes.
V Wonton noodle soup: add thin egg noodles to each bowl.

@ cheesecake | New York Cheesecake | 12 | 30 | 90 | milk, eggs, wheat | The classic New York cheesecake: dense, rich and tangy with cream cheese and sour cream, baked slowly in a water bath on a buttery graham cracker crust.
- 1 1/2 | cups | graham cracker crumbs (about 11 full sheets)
- 1/3 | cup | sugar, for the crust
- 6 | tbsp | unsalted butter, melted
- 2 | lb | full-fat cream cheese (four 8-oz blocks), softened
- 1 1/4 | cups | sugar
- 3 | tbsp | all-purpose flour
- 1/4 | tsp | salt
- 2 | tsp | vanilla extract
- 1 | tsp | lemon zest
- 1 | tbsp | fresh lemon juice
- 4 | | large eggs, at room temperature
- 1 | cup | full-fat sour cream, at room temperature
# Heat the oven to 350°F. Wrap the outside of a 9-inch springform pan in two layers of wide heavy-duty foil.
# Mix the graham cracker crumbs, 1/3 cup sugar and butter and press firmly into the bottom of the pan. Bake for 10 minutes, then cool. Lower the oven to 325°F and bring a kettle of water to a boil.
# Beat the cream cheese, 1 1/4 cups sugar, flour and salt on medium speed until completely smooth, about 2 minutes, scraping the bowl.
# Beat in the vanilla, lemon zest and juice. Add the eggs one at a time on low speed, mixing just until combined. Mix in the sour cream.
# Pour the filling over the crust. Set the pan in a roasting pan and pour boiling water into the roasting pan to come 1 inch up the sides.
# Bake for 75 to 85 minutes, until the edges are set and the center still jiggles slightly.
# Turn off the oven, prop the door open and leave the cheesecake inside for 1 hour. Then cool on a rack and refrigerate for at least 6 hours, or overnight.
T Have everything at room temperature. Cold cream cheese makes a lumpy batter.
T Mix on low once the eggs go in. Beating in air causes cracks.
T For clean slices, dip the knife in hot water and wipe it dry between cuts.
S Refrigerate for up to 5 days, or freeze whole or in slices for up to 2 months.
V Top with strawberry or cherry sauce just before serving.
