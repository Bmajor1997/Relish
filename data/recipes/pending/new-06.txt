- 1 1/4 | cups | all-purpose flour, plus more for rolling
- 1/2 | tsp | kosher salt, plus 1/2 tsp for the custard
- 1/2 | cup | cold unsalted butter, cubed
- 3 to 4 | tbsp | ice water
- 6 | oz | thick-cut smoked bacon, cut into 1/2-inch strips
- 3 | | large eggs
- 1 | | large egg yolk
- 1 1/4 | cups | heavy cream
- 1/2 | cup | whole milk
- 1/8 | tsp | ground nutmeg
- 1/4 | tsp | ground black pepper
# Make the pastry: mix the flour and salt, then rub in the butter with your fingertips until it looks like coarse crumbs with some pea-size pieces. Stir in just enough ice water for the dough to hold together. Flatten into a disk, wrap and chill for 1 hour.
# Roll the dough out on a floured counter and line a 9-inch tart pan or deep pie dish. Prick the base with a fork and chill for 15 minutes. Heat the oven to 400°F.

# Whisk the eggs, yolk, cream, milk, nutmeg, pepper and 1/2 teaspoon salt until smooth, without beating in air.
# Scatter the bacon in the warm pastry shell and pour in the custard.
# Bake for 35 to 40 minutes, until the edges are set but the center still wobbles slightly. Cool for at least 20 minutes before slicing.
T Blind-baking the crust first is what keeps the bottom crisp instead of soggy.
T Take the quiche out while the center still jiggles. It keeps cooking as it cools, and an overbaked custard turns rubbery.
S Bake up to a day ahead, refrigerate, and serve at room temperature or reheat at 325°F for 15 minutes.
V Add 1 cup grated Gruyère with the bacon, the way it's often made outside France.
V Use a 9-inch store-bought pie crust to save time.
