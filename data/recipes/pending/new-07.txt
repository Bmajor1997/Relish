- 1/4 | cup | lard or vegetable oil
- 1 | | orange, halved

# Heat the oven to 300°F. Toss the pork with the salt, cumin, oregano and pepper.
# Put the pork in a Dutch oven in a snug layer with the lard. Squeeze in the orange juice and add the squeezed halves, the onion, garlic, bay leaves, cinnamon stick, if using, and the water.

T Choose a well-marbled shoulder. The fat renders out and does the frying for you.
S Refrigerate the braised, shredded pork in its juices for up to 4 days. Crisp just before serving.
V Use the carnitas for burritos, tortas or rice bowls.
