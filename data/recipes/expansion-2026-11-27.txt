Ingredient-specific review:
Tangerine Pink Pepper Gin Collins is not verified nut-free. Pink peppercorn cross-reactivity with cashew/pistachio is documented in the primary study at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6676063/. Its nut-free label is set to review rather than fits in dietReview.json.

Packaged ingredients and alcohol formulations vary. Declared allergens describe the specified ingredients and cannot rule out cross-contact. Check product labels. Non-alcoholic suggestions are separate measured preparations; no alcohol-free claim is made about ordinary spirits or bitters.
